# Paid Service Policy

This Policy is a translation of the Korean original. If the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

Under Article 14 of the Terms of Service, this Policy sets out the rules for buying and using Paid Star Shards, withdrawing a purchase, and refunds.

## Article 1 (Payment Method)
Paid Star Shards can be bought only through Steam payments. The payment screen, payment methods and currency are provided by Steam.

## Article 2 (Products)
The products on sale are listed below. Prices and contents may change; the amount shown on the payment screen applies.

| Product | Contents | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Star Shards 300 | 300 Paid Star Shards | KRW 1,100 |
| Star Shards 1,000 | 1,000 Paid Star Shards | KRW 3,300 |
| Star Shards 3,000 | 3,000 Paid Star Shards | KRW 9,900 |
| Starlight Pass 30 days | 300 Paid Star Shards at once, plus 30 Free Star Shards and 1 Sealed Chest every day for 30 days | KRW 4,400 |

The Starlight Pass can be bought again as long as the remaining period does not exceed 60 days in total.

## Article 3 (Paid and Free Star Shards)
1. Star Shards bought with money are Paid Star Shards. Star Shards from the Pass's daily rewards, play or events are Free Star Shards.
2. When Star Shards are spent, Free Star Shards are used first, and Paid Star Shards are used in order of purchase, oldest first.
3. Star Shards and Game Items obtained with them are bound to the account and cannot be moved to another account.

## Article 4 (Withdrawal of Purchase)
1. A user may withdraw a purchase (cancel it) within 7 days of buying Paid Star Shards. Only unused Paid Star Shards can be withdrawn.
2. The Starlight Pass can be withdrawn only if no daily reward or Sealed Chest has been received and the Star Shards granted at once have not been used.
3. If a product differs from its description or was provided differently from the contract, the purchase can be withdrawn within 3 months of receipt, or within 30 days of learning of the difference.
4. To withdraw, request a refund through Steam or email {{email}} with your character name and the payment date and time. We confirm whether the Star Shards were used within 3 business days of your request and cancel the payment through Steam. Once the payment is cancelled, the corresponding Star Shards are reclaimed automatically.

Under Article 17(2) of the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, withdrawal is limited in the following cases. The Company notifies users of this before payment.

- Paid Star Shards already used (to the extent used)
- Draws, Sealed Chests, cosmetics, the Growth Pass, skill styles and the like already bought with Paid Star Shards
- Pass daily rewards or chests already received

## Article 5 (Cancellation by Minors)
If a user under 19 pays without the consent of a legal guardian, the user or the guardian may cancel the payment, either through Steam's refund process or by sending the payment details and proof of guardianship to {{email}}. Cancellation is not possible where the minor used deception to appear to be an adult or to have the guardian's consent.

## Article 6 (Refund Procedure)
1. Refunds are requested through Steam's refund process. When Steam processes a refund, the Company recovers the Paid Star Shards remaining from that order.
2. If Star Shards from a refunded order have already been spent, the amount spent remains as a debt. While a debt remains, Star Shards cannot be spent, and Star Shards received later repay the debt first.
3. Game Items obtained with refunded Star Shards may be recovered after review by the Company.
4. If a Starlight Pass is refunded, its remaining period ends. Daily rewards already received are not recovered.
5. If a charge was made in error by the Company, the full amount is refunded.

## Article 7 (Restrictions for Refund Abuse)
For repeated refund abuse or payment fraud (refunding after spending the Star Shards received, chargebacks and the like), the Company may impose a payment hold and an economy hold. Detailed standards follow Article 3 of the Operation Policy.

## Article 8 (Refunds When the Service Ends)
If the Service ends, the Company announces this at least 30 days in advance and refunds unused Paid Star Shards remaining at the end. How and when to apply is set out in the end-of-service notice.

## Article 9 (Probability Disclosure)
The probabilities of probability-based products, such as draws, Sealed Chests, cosmetic synthesis and gear enhancement, can be checked at any time in the in-game probability view and on the web page ([probability disclosure](/policies/en/probability)). Changes are announced at least 7 days in advance. The independence of attempts, the keeping of attempt records, and the correction of and compensation for probability display errors follow Article 14-2 of the Terms of Service.
