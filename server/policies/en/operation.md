# Operation Policy

This Policy is a translation of the Korean original. If the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

## Article 1 (Purpose and Scope)
Under Article 12 of the Terms of Service, this Policy sets out conduct prohibited in dotRPG, the standards for restrictions (sanctions), and the notice and appeal procedures. It applies to all in-game chat (including guild chat), character names, guild names and notices, party recruitment posts, trades (auction house, mail) and payments.

## Article 2 (Types of Sanctions)

| Type | Effect | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Warning | The breach is notified and recorded | None |
| Chat restriction | All chat and whispers are blocked | 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days, 30 days |
| Account suspension | Login is blocked | 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, permanent |
| Economy hold | Features that move currency (auction house, mail, trades) are blocked | Until the investigation ends |
| Payment hold | Paid Star Shards cannot be bought | Until the investigation ends |
| Name change request | The user is asked to change an inappropriate character name | Until changed |
| Guild name or notice change | The guild is asked to change an inappropriate guild name or notice, or an operator replaces the name with a temporary one and clears the notice | Until changed |
| Guild disbandment | The guild is disbanded. Its members cannot join another guild for 24 hours | None |

Permanent suspension is imposed only with the approval of the operations owner.

## Article 3 (Sanction Standards by Breach)
A repeated breach of the same type receives the next step. Serious breaches, or several types at once, may skip steps.

| Breach | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abuse, demeaning or hateful language | Chat restriction 1 day | Chat restriction 7 days | Suspension 7 days |
| Sexual content | Chat restriction 7 days | Suspension 7 days | Suspension 30 days |
| Spam | Warning | Chat restriction 1 day | Chat restriction 7 days |
| Advertising, fraud, promoting real-money trading | Suspension 7 days | Suspension 30 days | Permanent suspension |
| Impersonating operators | Suspension 7 days | Suspension 30 days | Permanent suspension |
| Inappropriate character name | Name change request | Suspension 1 day and name change request | Suspension 7 days and name change request |
| Inappropriate guild name or notice | Warning and guild name or notice change request | Operator changes the guild name or notice and suspends the guild leader for 1 day | Guild disbandment and suspension of the guild leader for 7 days |
| Real-money trading (RMT) | Suspension 30 days and recovery of unfair gains | Permanent suspension | |
| Selling, lending or sharing accounts | Suspension 30 days | Permanent suspension | |
| Illegal programs, macros | Suspension 30 days and recovery of unfair gains | Permanent suspension | |
| Exploiting bugs or errors | Suspension 7 days and recovery of unfair gains | Suspension 30 days and recovery of unfair gains | Permanent suspension |
| Stealing another's account, hacking | Permanent suspension | | |
| Payment fraud, refund abuse | Payment hold and economy hold | Suspension 30 days | Permanent suspension |

Users who find a bug or error must report it immediately to {{email}}. Continuing to use a known bug without reporting it counts as exploitation. Users who report it and stop using it are not sanctioned.

## Article 4 (Automatic Sanctions)
The game applies the following automatic sanctions. Accounts are never suspended automatically.

- Banned words in chat are masked with `*` before sending. The server stores only the masked message, never the original.
- Messages that try to hide banned words with spaces or inserted symbols are not sent.
- 5 banned-word hits within 10 minutes restrict chat for 10 minutes.
- Sending the same message 3 times in a row restricts chat for 10 seconds. 5 such restrictions within 10 minutes restrict chat for 10 minutes.
- From the third automatic chat restriction within 24 hours, the restriction lasts 24 hours.
- Chat is limited to 30 messages per minute and 1 message per second.

## Article 5 (Character Name Rules)
1. Character names are 2 to 8 characters of Hangul, Latin letters or digits.
2. A name already used by another user cannot be used (case-insensitive).
3. Names impersonating the operators or the Company (operator, administrator, GM, admin, official and their variants) and names containing abuse, sexual or hateful expressions cannot be created.
4. Inappropriate names created by evading the automatic check receive a name change request or a sanction under Article 3.

## Article 5-2 (Guild Name and Notice Rules)
1. Guild names are 2 to 12 characters, and a name already used by another guild cannot be used (case-insensitive). Notices are up to 60 characters.
2. The impersonation, abuse, sexual and hateful expression standards of Article 5(3) and the banned-word check apply equally to guild names and notices.
3. Reports and sanctions concerning a guild name or notice follow Articles 3 and 6; notice of the sanction and appeals are handled with the guild leader under Articles 7 and 8.

## Article 6 (Reports)
1. Users can report other users from the chat window. Report reasons are: abuse or demeaning language, spam, advertising or fraud, suspected illegal program, and other.
2. Recent conversation is attached automatically as evidence. For whispers, only those between the reporter and the reported user are attached.
3. Up to 5 reports per hour and 20 per day can be made.
4. False reports made to harass others are sanctioned as abusive language under Article 3.
5. Users can block other users. Blocking stops chat, whispers and invitations from that user.

## Article 7 (Notice of Sanctions)
1. A sanction is notified immediately if the user is online, or at the next login otherwise.
2. A suspended user who tries to log in is shown the time the suspension ends.
3. The notice states the type, reason and duration of the sanction.

## Article 8 (Appeals)
1. A user who disagrees with a sanction may appeal to {{email}} within 30 days of receiving the notice.
2. Please include the character name, the type and time of the sanction, and the reason for the appeal.
3. The Company reports the result within 7 business days of receiving the appeal. If the review takes longer, it gives the reason and the expected schedule.
4. If a sanction is found to be unjustified, it is lifted immediately and any resulting loss is compensated.

## Article 9 (Advance Notice of Probability Changes)
Changes to the probabilities of probability-based items are announced in-game and on the web page at least 7 days in advance. The in-game probability view and the web page always show the current probabilities and the version of the rate table.

## Article 10 (Changes to This Policy)
Changes to this Policy are announced in-game and on the web page at least 7 days before they take effect.
